BoxingTimer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

BoxingTimer ("the app") is a round timer for boxing and combat sports training. This page explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data stored on your device

Your training settings and any profiles you save (round/rest durations, number of rounds, profile names) are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us and is not linked to your identity.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and other data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can remove ads permanently via the one-time "Remove Ads" purchase in Settings.

Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details — that is handled entirely by Google Play.

Permissions

The app requests notification permission to show a persistent notification with timer controls while a workout is running in the background. It does not access your camera, contacts, location, or microphone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to usmoder@gmail.com.
